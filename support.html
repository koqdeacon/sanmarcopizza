Common questions

I need a promotional code to sign up. Membership is invite-only — ask the shop for an active code.

How do I cancel an order? Open “My Orders” and swipe an upcoming order to cancel, up to 2 hours before your pick-up time.

How do I delete my account? Go to My Profile → Delete My Account.
