Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the San Marco Pizza app ("the app") collects, why, and your choices. The app is operated by San Marco Pizza ("we", "us").

Information we collect

  • Email address — used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Name — an optional display name you provide at signup or in your profile.
  • Profile photo — an optional avatar you choose to upload.
  • Order information — the pizzas you order, the chosen pickup slot, and order status, linked to your account.

How we use your information

We use this information solely to operate the app: to authenticate you, to process and fulfil your pizza orders, to show your order history, and to send you reminders about upcoming pickups. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How your information is stored and shared

Your data is stored on our behalf by Supabase, our backend and database provider, which acts as our data processor. We do not sell your personal information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device to remind you when your order is ready for pickup. You can disable these at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account at any time from My Profile → Delete My Account inside the app. This removes your profile, name, avatar, and cancels any upcoming orders. This action cannot be undone.

Data retention

We retain your account and order information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at sanmarcopizza@icloud.com.
