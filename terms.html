Terms

Last updated: 11 September 2026

This is a hobby project, not a commercial business. San Marco Pizza and the app that runs it were built for fun, to cook for friends and neighbors, and to test the software itself. It isn't a registered company or a professional food-service operation, and these terms are written accordingly — plain and short, not a law firm's contract.

What this is

The app lets a pizzeria (or, for other affiliates using the same app, whoever runs one) announce "Pizza Days" and "Bakers Days," take pre-orders, and hand out food at pickup. Using it means you're signing up to help someone cook and to show up for what you ordered — not entering a retail contract with a company.

Ordering and pickup

  • Placing an order is a commitment to pick it up at the chosen time or window. If you can't make it, please cancel in the app before the cancellation deadline shown on the event, so the food (and anyone's time) isn't wasted.
  • Menus, availability, and pickup times are set by whoever is running that event and can change or be cancelled — this is home/community cooking, not a restaurant with guaranteed inventory.

Payment

Depending on the pizzeria, you pay in cash at pickup or by card through Stripe, our payment processor (see the Privacy Policy for how that works). Card payments are only ever meant to cover the cost of ingredients and the event — this isn't a for-profit retail sale.

Accounts and codes

You need an account to order, and you need to join a specific pizzeria (by QR code or invite code) to see its menu. Don't share join codes outside the group they're meant for, and don't use the app to order on someone else's behalf without their knowledge.

No guarantees

The app is provided as-is, run by someone testing and improving it, not a company offering a service-level guarantee. We try to keep orders, pickup times, and notifications accurate, but things can go wrong — a Pizza Day can be cancelled, the app can have bugs, and we can't promise it'll always work perfectly. We're not liable for food allergies or dietary needs beyond what's listed in the app — always check the ingredients list yourself if you have an allergy.

Changes

These terms may change as the app (and this hobby) evolves. If San Marco Pizza ever becomes a real registered business, this page will be replaced with proper terms and an Impressum.
